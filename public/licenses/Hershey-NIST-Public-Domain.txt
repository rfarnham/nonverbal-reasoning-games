SUBTRACTION FLASH TRACE NUMERALS — SOURCE AND PUBLIC-DOMAIN NOTICE

The normalized single-line coordinates for digits 2–9 in
app/lab/subtraction-flash/trace-geometry.ts are adapted from the plain print
row of “Hershey fonts, digits.svg” on Wikimedia Commons:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hershey_fonts,_digits.svg

Pinned page revision:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Hershey_fonts,_digits.svg&oldid=1225175617

Original SVG:
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/94/Hershey_fonts%2C_digits.svg

Retrieved 2026-08-07. The paths were normalized to the game’s tracing surface;
the 4 was opened slightly to match the supplied child-handwriting sample, and
the 5 was split at its natural pen lift so one- and two-stroke forms work.

The Wikimedia file page identifies the Hershey font data as public domain in
the United States because it is a work of a United States Federal Government
employee, specifically the National Institute of Standards and Technology,
under 17 U.S.C. § 105. The browser uses only the locally bundled normalized
coordinates and makes no request to Wikimedia or any other font service.

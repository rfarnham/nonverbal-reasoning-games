Five explicit checks

What you verify for every item

  1. Question boundaryOne complete question; nothing clipped or merged.
  2. ChoicesEvery choice is present, ordered, and faithfully parsed.
  3. Official answerThe answer and status match the cited answer key.
  4. Diagram and cropAll necessary visual information is complete and legible.

Do not fill gaps by guessing

Content gaps are not parser repairs

A missing official solution, unavailable OCR confidence, or unknown published point tier must stay recorded as a content gap. Verify what the evidence supports; never invent the missing value.

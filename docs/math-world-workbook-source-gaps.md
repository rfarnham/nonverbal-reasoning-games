# Original workbook source audit

Updated 2026-09-29. This report tracks the remaining source limitations for the 32 teaching worlds.

The selected curriculum has 680 questions, including 367 whose source cards were synthesized from the Oasis question corpus. Of those 367 cards, 333 now have verified original-paper or original-workbook replacements (60 existing plus 273 recovered). Another two non-Oasis questions retain their existing original overrides, for 335 total print-asset overrides. The 34 remaining Oasis cards below retain the existing card; they are not claimed to be original exam layouts.

Replacement selection was checked against the playable question, including prompt conditions, diagram state, and ordered answer choices. Equal answer keys alone were not accepted as evidence of equivalence. Original bilingual text, illustration placement and typography are retained. Unrelated neighbors, page headers/footers and tier headings are excluded. Source PDF hashes, page numbers and crop rectangles are recorded with the manifest. The existing question IDs, scoring and curriculum are unchanged.

Two source clarifications preserve an explicit game rule omitted from an otherwise matching paper: 2018 grades 1–2 question 10 (both squares may rotate), and 2013 grades 3–4 question 6 (candy pieces may not be broken). The original stamp artwork in 2017 grades 1–2 question 21 uses a different stamp orientation but retains uniquely correct choice E and the same ordered window/door arrangements. The 2016 grades 1–2 question 9 stairway source has been checked by counting each ordered black/white ball sequence despite a void source-key metadata flag.
